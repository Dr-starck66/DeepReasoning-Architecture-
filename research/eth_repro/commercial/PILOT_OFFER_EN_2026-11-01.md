# ASTRA ProofLab Ω — Fixed-price Evidence Audit Pilot
**Offer status:** Proposed commercial service, subject to buyer acceptance and signed statement of work. **November 1, 2026.**

## Why buy this
An independent, bounded audit of the evidence that connects a technical claim to the exact public source revision, executable tests, formal statements and reproducibility receipts. Independent verification of a claimed CI run is different from certifying the scientific conclusion or software system.

## Pilot specification
- **Fee proposed:** EUR 2,900 excluding applicable taxes, fixed scope. The quote is not a signed contract, invoice or booked revenue.
- **Target customer:** research / AI / formal-methods teams publishing verifiable research or proof-carrying software.
- **Input scope:** one mutually selected repository at one immutable commit; up to five explicit claims; up to one primary CI workflow and five Lean files (where applicable). Public artifacts preferred. Private data or secrets require a separate secure agreement and approved exchange method.
- **Method:** signed-off claim list; content SHA-256; immutable Git commit and CI-run binding; inspect Lean kernel/axiom dependencies when present; minimum two deliberate negative controls; independent reproduction where tooling and inputs permit.
- **Deliverables:** 1) evidence ledger JSON with PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL/UNVERIFIED per claim; 2) concise PDF or HTML decision report; 3) reproducible checks and exact commands/commit IDs; 4) prioritized remediation list; 5) one review call.
- **Acceptance:** deliverables supplied for the scoped five claims and explanatory limitations explicitly documented. A negative technical verdict still counts as a completed independent audit.
- **Schedule proposed:** five business days from agreement plus receipt of all agreed inputs.
- **Excluded:** independent mathematical proof of unpublished general theorems, legal safety certification, cybersecurity penetration testing, regulatory approvals, and commercial valuation. Zero guarantee of PASS.
- **Commercial terms:** payment schedule, applicable VAT/taxes, legal contracting party, permitted reuse, liability and confidentiality are to be specified in a signed quote before any work. No expenditure or paid service is activated by this document.
